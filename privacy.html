Privacy Policy

Utuscribe, LLC operates communication services including Responder AI and ResponderLite. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Effective date

February 16, 2026

Information we collect

  • Phone numbers and message content used to provide service-related communication.
  • Call and message metadata, including timestamps, basic delivery status, and routing data.
  • Contact information voluntarily submitted by end users or participating businesses.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and facilitate customer-care communication.
  • To coordinate follow-up, intake, scheduling, and callback communication between callers and participating businesses.
  • To operate, troubleshoot, maintain, and improve service reliability and security.

ResponderLite SMS notice

ResponderLite is a missed-call follow-up and customer-care messaging service provided by Utuscribe, LLC on behalf of participating local service businesses.

When an individual calls a participating business, the caller may be offered the option to receive SMS follow-up regarding that inquiry. Consent is collected through the call flow. For example, the caller may hear: "For faster service, press 1 to receive text messages about your request. Message and data rates may apply."

A caller who presses 1 is providing prior express consent to receive customer-care text messages related to that inquiry. After consent is collected, Utuscribe, LLC may send one or more conversational follow-up text messages through ResponderLite on behalf of the participating business. These messages may relate to the missed call, service request, intake, scheduling, or callback coordination.

Messages are sent only in response to a customer-initiated interaction with a participating business. ResponderLite messages are customer-care and conversational in nature. No marketing or promotional messages are sent through ResponderLite.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase.

Message frequency, opt-out, and help

Message frequency varies based on the customer's interaction with the participating business. Message and data rates may apply depending on the user's carrier plan.

End users may opt out at any time by replying STOP. End users may request assistance by replying HELP.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share information with service providers and telecommunications providers solely as necessary to operate, support, secure, and deliver the service.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Participating business responsibilities

Participating businesses are responsible for configuring their phone routing and customer communication practices in compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and carrier requirements.
