Privacy Policy

Utuscribe, LLC operates communication services including Responder AI and ResponderLite. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Effective date

February 16, 2026

Information we collect

  • Phone numbers and message content used to provide service communication.
  • Call and message metadata (timestamps, basic delivery status, and routing data).
  • Contact information you voluntarily submit (for example via a request form).

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and facilitate customer-service communication.
  • To coordinate follow-up and scheduling between callers and participating businesses.
  • To operate, troubleshoot, and improve service reliability and security.

ResponderLite specific notice

ResponderLite is a missed-call follow-up service. When an individual places a phone call to a participating business and does not reach a live representative, an automated text message may be sent related directly to that call to collect basic service details and coordinate a callback.

ResponderLite messages are customer-care and conversational in nature. No marketing or promotional messages are sent through ResponderLite.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. We may share information with service providers used to deliver the service (for example, telecommunications providers), solely to operate the service.

Opt-out and help

You may reply STOP to unsubscribe from messages. You may reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier plan.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: support@utuscribe.com

If you are a participating business using ResponderLite, you are responsible for configuring your phone routing and ensuring your customer communications comply with applicable laws and policies.
