ResponderLite

ResponderLite: missed-call follow-up that converts.

ResponderLite helps participating service businesses respond to missed calls by sending an immediate, customer-care text message to the caller and collecting basic details for a fast callback. No marketing. No spam. Just clean, reliable intake.

How it works

  • A customer calls your business and does not reach a live representative.
  • ResponderLite sends a follow-up text related directly to that missed call.
  • The customer replies with their name and what they need.
  • You receive a summary with the callback number and details.

ResponderLite is designed for customer care and appointment coordination. It does not send promotional messages.

Fast Response

Follow up on missed calls immediately so customers do not drift to competitors.

Clear Intake

Collect the basics (name + request) so your callback is efficient and informed.

On/Off Control

Toggle service when needed. In STOP mode, calls can pass through to your business line.

Opt-out: reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.
